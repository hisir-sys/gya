# Chapter 6 — The Green Promise

**District:** New Town Eco Park  
**Lead:** Eli Cross  
**Theme:** Shared spaces and practical compromise

A volunteer garden group and a weekend events team disagree about how to use a lakeside lawn. Eli helps them create a schedule that protects the garden while keeping the park welcoming.

## Mission beats
1. **Two Calendars** — Hear both groups explain their plans.
2. **The Quiet Hour** — Walk the park and identify less busy times.
3. **A Better Layout** — Mark a visitor route that avoids planted beds.
4. **The Open Garden** — Attend the first shared event.

## Choice
Reserve more time for community gardening or public events.

## Continuity
Both choices preserve access; later dialogue acknowledges the agreed schedule.
