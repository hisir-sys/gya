# Chapter 2 — The Last Tram

**District:** Central Kolkata / College Street  
**Lead:** Nia Vale  
**Theme:** Public memory versus private convenience

A retired tram conductor, Arun, is trying to preserve a route map before a depot is redeveloped. Nia discovers that the map also records neighbourhood services that quietly disappeared.

## Mission beats
1. **A Route in Pencil** — Meet Arun and inspect his annotated route map.
2. **The Depot Ledger** — Compare the old route notes with the public notice.
3. **One More Stop** — Help residents record the stops that still matter to them.
4. **Keep the Line** — Deliver the collected testimony to the community archive.

## Choice
Publish the full archive, including disputed entries, or publish a verified public copy while keeping private resident notes restricted.

## Continuity
The choice changes later dialogue from Arun and the archive volunteers. Neither outcome blocks the main campaign.
