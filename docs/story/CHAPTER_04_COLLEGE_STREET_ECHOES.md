# Chapter 4 — College Street Echoes

**District:** College Street  
**Lead:** Nia Vale  
**Theme:** Knowledge, access and preservation

A second-hand bookshop is preparing to close. Its owner has a handwritten catalogue that could help a nearby student reading room rebuild its collection.

## Mission beats
1. **The Blue Notebook** — Find the shop and learn why the catalogue matters.
2. **A Shelf for Everyone** — Visit the reading room and identify missing subjects.
3. **The Missing Edition** — Trace a mislabelled box through two book stalls.
4. **Open Catalogue** — Help publish a community catalogue.

## Choice
Prioritise rare books for preservation or affordable everyday reading material.

## Continuity
The reading room can be referenced as a recurring story location.
