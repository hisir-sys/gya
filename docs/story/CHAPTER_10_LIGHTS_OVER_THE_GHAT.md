# Chapter 10 — Lights Over the Ghat

**District:** Hooghly riverfront  
**Lead:** Jax, Nia and Eli  
**Theme:** Collaboration without a single hero

Community projects from earlier chapters are invited to share a public evening by the river. A scheduling conflict asks the three protagonists to coordinate calmly.

## Mission beats
1. **Three Invitations** — Revisit contacts and confirm what each group wants to contribute.
2. **The Shared Schedule** — Build a timetable that gives every group space.
3. **A Walkable Evening** — Check the visitor route, signs and quiet areas.
4. **City of Many Voices** — Open the event and let the community lead.

## Choice
Give the closing speech to a long-time resident or to youth volunteers.

## Finale
The ending reflects choices from Chapters 2–9. Both outcomes celebrate a shared city rather than one hero.
