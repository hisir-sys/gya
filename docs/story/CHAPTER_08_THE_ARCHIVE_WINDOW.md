# Chapter 8 — The Archive Window

**District:** Salt Lake Sector V  
**Lead:** Nia Vale  
**Theme:** Responsible information sharing

An office archivist finds old project photographs mixed with private employee records. Nia helps separate public history from personal information.

## Mission beats
1. **Box 17** — Meet archivist Ishan and inspect the collection inventory.
2. **Public or Private** — Sort material into public, permission-needed and private groups.
3. **The Consent List** — Request permission for selected photographs.
4. **A Clear Record** — Deliver a carefully labelled public archive.

## Choice
Publish only material with explicit permission or publish a smaller public timeline and keep the rest restricted.

## Continuity
Later dialogue recognises the player's careful archival choices.
