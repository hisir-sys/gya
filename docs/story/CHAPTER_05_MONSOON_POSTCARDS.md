# Chapter 5 — Monsoon Postcards

**District:** South Kolkata Lakes  
**Lead:** Jax Mercer  
**Theme:** Patience and community care

A local photographer's monsoon exhibition has lost its captions. Jax helps reconnect the photographs with the people and places behind them.

## Mission beats
1. **The Uncaptioned Frame** — Meet photographer Tara at the lakeside pavilion.
2. **Names Behind the Rain** — Visit nearby locations and ask residents about the images.
3. **The Dry Print Room** — Deliver the recovered captions to the community hall.
4. **Postcards Home** — Help arrange the exhibition in a route visitors can follow.

## Choice
Arrange the exhibition chronologically or by neighbourhood voices.

## Continuity
The chosen arrangement is reflected in later journal summaries.
