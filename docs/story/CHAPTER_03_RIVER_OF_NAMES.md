# Chapter 3 — River of Names

**District:** Hooghly riverfront  
**Lead:** Eli Cross  
**Theme:** Belonging and shared history

A community noticeboard beside the river is due to be removed. Eli helps the people who use it recover the stories and practical information pinned there.

## Mission beats
1. **The Noticeboard** — Meet volunteer organiser Farida and catalogue the board's sections.
2. **Names on the Steps** — Ask regular visitors for permission to preserve their contributions.
3. **A Place to Keep It** — Compare three possible community locations for a replacement.
4. **The River Remembers** — Open the new board with the residents.

## Choice
Place the board at the busy ghat entrance or beside the quieter community reading room.

## Continuity
The selected location becomes a recurring landmark in future story descriptions.
