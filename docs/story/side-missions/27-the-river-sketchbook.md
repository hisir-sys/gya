# Side Mission — The River Sketchbook

- **District:** Hooghly riverfront
- **Lead:** Jax Mercer
- **Theme:** Art and observation
- **Status:** Narrative design brief; not yet connected to the playable mission runtime.

## Premise
An art student has sketched the same river view across changing seasons and wants to invite others to contribute.

## Mission beats
1. Meet the artist
2. find a public display location
3. collect opt-in sketches
4. arrange a small exhibition.

## Player choice
Arrange by season or by each artist's viewpoint.
