# Side Mission 05 — The Rain Gauge

- **District:** South Kolkata Lakes
- **Lead:** Jax Mercer
- **Theme:** Public knowledge
- **Status:** Narrative design brief; not yet connected to the playable mission runtime.

## Premise
A neighbourhood group is comparing rain measurements but its records use inconsistent units and dates.

## Mission beats
1. Meet the volunteers
2. check the instruments
3. organise the notebooks
4. publish a clear monthly summary.

## Player choice
Publish raw readings alongside the summary or publish only verified averages.

## Implementation notes
