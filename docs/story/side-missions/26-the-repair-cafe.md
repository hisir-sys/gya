# Side Mission — The Repair Café

- **District:** Salt Lake Sector V
- **Lead:** Eli Cross
- **Theme:** Practical skills
- **Status:** Narrative design brief; not yet connected to the playable mission runtime.

## Premise
A group of office workers wants to host a weekend event where neighbours can learn basic repair skills.

## Mission beats
1. Meet the organisers
2. confirm suitable tables
3. prepare a sign-up board
4. host the first repair café.

## Player choice
Focus on household items or small electronics.
