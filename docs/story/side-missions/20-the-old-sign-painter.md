# Side Mission 20 — The Old Sign Painter

- **District:** North Kolkata
- **Lead:** Nia Vale
- **Theme:** Craft continuity
- **Status:** Narrative design brief; not yet connected to the playable mission runtime.

## Premise
A sign painter is preparing to retire and wants to pass on lettering patterns to younger artists.

## Mission beats
1. Visit the studio
2. catalogue sample alphabets
3. arrange a demonstration
4. archive the patterns with permission.

## Player choice
Create a printed booklet or a public digital gallery.
