# Chapter 9 — Market of Morning

**District:** North Kolkata market lanes  
**Lead:** Eli Cross  
**Theme:** Neighbourhood resilience

Small shops are planning a shared market day, but their hand-drawn stall map is confusing visitors. Eli helps create a clearer, accessible layout.

## Mission beats
1. **A Map on Cardboard** — Meet the vendors and inspect the draft.
2. **Room to Pass** — Walk the route and note narrow or confusing sections.
3. **Signs in Two Languages** — Prepare Bengali and English direction cards.
4. **The Market Opens** — Test the route with visitors and collect feedback.

## Choice
Group stalls by product type or by participating lane.

## Continuity
The selected layout appears in the organiser's journal entry.
