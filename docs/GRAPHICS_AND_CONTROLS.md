# Graphics and Controls Guide

## Graphics presets

Use **GRAPHICS** in the in-game toolbar to cycle through the available presets:

- **PERFORMANCE** — reduced pixel ratio and smaller shadows for integrated graphics.
- **HIGH** — balanced image quality and performance.
- **ULTRA** — higher pixel ratio, larger shadows, and atmospheric dust.
- **EXTREME** — highest configured quality. This can be demanding on older laptops and phones.

ULTRA and EXTREME monitor frame rate in short windows. If performance stays low, the renderer reduces pixel ratio and shadow-map resolution; when performance recovers, it gradually restores quality. The FPS readout is a measurement, not a guarantee of a particular frame rate.

## Camera controls

- Move the mouse while the game has pointer lock to look around.
- Scroll the mouse wheel over the game to zoom the follow camera in or out.
- Press **+** or **=** to zoom in and **-** to zoom out.
- Use **RESET CAMERA** to restore the default angle and zoom.
- Press **Escape** to release pointer lock or pause.

## Keyboard controls

- **WASD / arrow keys** — move or drive.
- **Shift** — sprint on foot or nitro while driving forward.
- **Space** — handbrake while driving; interact/advance dialogue on foot.
- **E** — interact.
- **F** — enter or exit the vehicle.
- **T** — start or cancel a street sprint.
- **1–3** — switch protagonists.
- **R** — recover at the current checkpoint.

## Touch controls

On touch-first devices, an on-screen pad provides movement, interaction, vehicle entry/exit, sprint/nitro, and braking. Hold directional/action buttons for continuous input; tap E or F for one-shot actions.

## Manual validation

Test each preset on at least one integrated-GPU laptop and one modern desktop. Record browser, GPU, display resolution, preset, average FPS, and whether adaptive scaling engaged. Test wheel zoom, keyboard zoom, pointer-lock release, window focus loss, and touch controls on a real touchscreen. Do not mark these tests as passed until they have been performed.
