# Neon State — Story Bible

## Creative direction

Neon State is an original urban sandbox about loyalty, ambition, and the cost of trying to escape a city that remembers everything. The first playable build uses a top-down 2D presentation so the core loop can be tested before moving to a more capable engine or adding original 3D assets.

## The three protagonists

### Jax Mercer — the wheelman
A gifted driver who takes small courier jobs after a risky delivery costs him his place in a legitimate racing team. His strengths are vehicle handling, fast escapes, and improvisation.

### Nia Vale — the strategist
A meticulous former logistics analyst who understands how the city's businesses and transport networks connect. Her missions focus on planning, surveillance-style observation, and finding alternative routes.

### Eli Cross — the fixer
A resourceful neighborhood mediator who knows the people behind the storefronts. His missions emphasize conversations, negotiation, access, and solving problems without escalating every situation.

These characters are original creations and are not based on GTA V protagonists.

## City

The game takes place in **Port Meridian**, a fictional coastal city divided into:
- **Downtown:** offices, civic buildings, construction sites, and busy intersections.
- **Uptown:** residential blocks, hillside roads, and small parks.
- **Waterfront:** docks, warehouses, repair yards, and ferry terminals.
- **Old Market:** narrow streets, local businesses, and community gathering places.
- **Industrial Belt:** depots, freight roads, and utility facilities.

The current prototype draws a simplified abstract city grid. District identity, landmarks, interiors, and a detailed map are planned additions.

## Chapter 1 — First Contact

1. **A Quiet Pickup:** Jax is asked to collect a sealed case from a waterfront garage. The objective introduces movement and the mission marker.
2. **The Route That Wasn't There:** Nia spots a road closure and finds a safer route through Downtown. The player learns that routes and choices can affect a mission.
3. **A Familiar Favor:** Eli visits a local mechanic to resolve a dispute over a missing delivery. Dialogue and interaction systems are introduced.
4. **Three Sides of the Same Job:** The protagonists discover that their separate tasks are connected to the same redevelopment deal.
5. **The Meeting Point:** The trio meets at the waterfront and agrees to investigate who is forcing small businesses out of the district.

## Future campaign arc

- **Chapter 2 — Crossed Wires:** follow the supply chain behind the redevelopment pressure.
- **Chapter 3 — The Long Way Home:** choose between a risky shortcut and a slower route that protects a local contact.
- **Chapter 4 — Open Ledger:** gather evidence from several districts and decide who should receive it.
- **Chapter 5 — Port in a Storm:** a multi-character finale whose outcome depends on earlier choices.

## Gameplay pillars

- Freely explore the city between missions.
- Switch among the three protagonists when the game state allows it.
- Drive original fictional vehicles.
- Discover mission markers, local events, and district landmarks.
- Make choices that can change later dialogue and mission availability.
- Save progress and resume a campaign.

## Implementation notes

The current web prototype implements basic movement, character switching, simple vehicle entry/exit, a minimap, a gold mission marker, and a starter objective. Later story beats in this document are design plans, not yet implemented gameplay. Use original writing, original names, and properly licensed or self-created assets throughout.
